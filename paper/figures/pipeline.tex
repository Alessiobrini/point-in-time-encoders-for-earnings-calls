\caption{From the corpus to the uses of the vintages. For each cutoff year $Y$ from 2010 to 2024, every pretraining recipe reads only text dated on or before 31 December of $Y$. Our two families read trainable calls, ChronoBERT reads general text, and ChronoBERT + DAPT reads both, at 2010, 2017 and 2024 only. Contrastive fine-tuning trains on question-answer pairs from the trainable calls, and the registry records the cutoff so that a query dated before it cannot use the vintage. Held-out calls supply every retrieval question and enter no stage of training other than the fit of the 2010 tokenizer.}
